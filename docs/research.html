Evidence over folklore.

The guide combines the manufacturer's documentation, an inspected official source snapshot, and release-specific community research. Its interactive models are original teaching tools. None of the research used a physical FM-1.

What each kind of evidence establishes

EvidenceUseful forDoes not establish
Official board sourcePeripheral configuration, API contracts, buffer sizes, input scanning and expected pin assignments.The complete analog schematic, exact DAC identity, or every shipping board revision.
Official application sourceControl logic, MIDI routing, output mixing, storage and the native binary boundary.The hidden native engine's implementation or bit-identical browser audio.
Manufacturer product page / manualPhysical interface and advertised product characteristics.Up-to-date capabilities of every newer firmware.
Maintainer source / releasesSpecific implementation and documented support in a named version.Independent validation on your unit, or a universal migration route.
This site's sound labAudible intuition for sinusoidal FM, ratios, index and an amplitude envelope.FM-1 presets, exact six-operator behavior, native performance or converter quality.

Resolved distinctions

  • Stock V16 ≠ FM1 Open V14. Different projects and version sequences. Stock's updated capabilities come from its own release announcement.
  • Open application ≠ open native DSP. FM1 Open hardware builds link a prebuilt engine; browser builds compile public C synthesis code.
  • 10 kHz tick ≠ 10 kHz full scan. The scanner cycles across eleven columns.
  • 44,100 vs 44,118 Hz. The nominal engine rate and the driver's documented hardware timing rate differ slightly. Both give about 2.90 ms per 128-frame block.
  • Parts ≠ voices. Tracks hold instrument/sequencer state. Voices are sounding notes and may share a budget.
  • Known flash variants matter. UC25HQ80 support is release-specific. Similar firmware ancestry does not guarantee matching loaders or project formats.

Updating the guide

Verify the destination repository and exact commit before changing a capability claim. Keep version-dependent facts with their source. Preserve uncertainty labels unless new primary evidence resolves them. A successful build or browser simulation is not a substitute for hardware testing.
